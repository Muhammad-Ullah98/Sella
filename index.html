FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things sellers ask before they start with Sella.

How does Sella know my prices and stock?
It only uses the product list you add. Your real products, prices, sizes and rules. It never makes up a price or says something is in stock when it is not. If it is not in your list, it asks you instead of guessing.
Does it work on both WhatsApp and Instagram?
Yes. One helper works on both, so you do not set anything up twice. Buyers get the same fast replies wherever they message you.
Can it handle Urdu, English, and Roman Urdu?
Yes. It understands the mix of Urdu, English and Roman Urdu that buyers really use in one message. It replies in the same style the buyer wrote in.
How does COD confirmation work?
When a buyer places a cash on delivery order, Sella checks the item, address and delivery time, and flags anything unclear before you ship. This is how you stop fake orders and save money on returns.
What happens with complaints or tricky messages?
Hard cases like complaints and refunds are sent straight to you with the full chat. Sella does the busy work. You handle your customers.
How long does setup take?
About 30 minutes. Add your products once, connect your WhatsApp and Instagram, and start. No code, nothing technical.
Can I take over a conversation myself?
Any time. Jump into any chat and Sella steps back for that one. You stay in full control of the customers you want to handle yourself.
Do I need a card to start?
No. Start free with a 14 day trial, no card needed. You only pay once Sella is already getting you sales.
